Common questions

Useful before you write.

These answers are intentionally policy-safe in the prototype. Final timing, exchange, warranty, shipping, and personalization details will come from verified MONOLITH policies rather than assumptions.

Where should I start if I am unsure about ring size?
Use the dedicated sizing guide first, then contact support if you are deciding between sizes or widths. The final guide will explain MONOLITH’s verified measuring and fit process.
Can I contact you about an order that is already placed?
Yes. Choose “Existing order” in the form and include the order number and checkout email so the request can be matched quickly.
What should I send for a warranty or finish question?
Include the product name or order number, a short description of what happened, and clear photographs of the ring from the relevant angles.
Where will shipping, returns, and engraving rules live?
Each will have a dedicated policy/help page, with the most important product-specific limitations also shown on the product page before purchase.
