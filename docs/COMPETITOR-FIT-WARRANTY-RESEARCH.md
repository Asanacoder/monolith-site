# 20 Additional Brands

## 1. Manly Bands

**Model**
- One exchange during the first 30 days.
- Engraved rings are final sale and cannot be returned or exchanged.
- Brand explicitly recommends ordering unengraved if uncertain about size.
- Lifetime manufacturing warranty covers manufacturing defects, not weight gain/loss, normal wear, loss, or theft.

**Interesting tactic**
- They encourage a customer to confirm fit first, then use the one exchange to send the ring back for engraving.
- This separates fit certainty from permanent personalization.

**Source**
- https://manlybands.com/pages/returns-exchanges
- https://manlybands.com/pages/warranty

---

## 2. Modern Gents

**Model**
- 30-day returns.
- 30-day exchanges, including wrong size or style.
- Lifetime warranty on products.
- Returned/exchanged rings that are worn or not in new condition may incur a 20% restocking fee.

**Interesting tactic**
- Very simple, low-friction policy language: returns, exchanges, and lifetime warranty are presented as three large reassurance pillars.

**Source**
- https://modgents.com/pages/returns
- https://checkout.modgents.com/pages/guarantee

---

## 3. Northern Royal

**Model**
- 90-day returns.
- 120-day free size exchange / resizing window.
- After 120 days, a $30 deductible allows another size exchange.
- No stated limit on later $30 size exchanges.
- Damage replacement is free during the first year.
- After the first year, damage replacement uses a $45 deductible.
- Wedding-date policy can delay the start of return/exchange/warranty clocks until after the wedding date.

**Interesting tactic**
- One of the clearest examples of turning sizing into a long-term service.
- The **Wedding Policy** is especially relevant to MONOLITH because buyers often purchase long before the ceremony.

**Source**
- https://northernroyal.com/policies/refund-policy
- https://northernroyal.com/pages/limited-lifetime-warranty

---

## 4. Larson Jewelers

**Model**
- Extensive lifetime warranty on wedding rings.
- Warranty can cover repair/replacement even when damage was the wearer's fault, depending on the ring.
- Lifetime sizing is emphasized: if finger size changes, the ring can be exchanged for a different size.
- Larson states that size changes can be handled as many times as needed under applicable warranty terms.
- Engraving does not void the warranty, but engraving on a new replacement ring is charged again.

**Interesting tactic**
- Strong separation between **ring replacement value** and **personalization value**.

**Source**
- https://www.larsonjewelers.com/pages/faq

## 5. Triton Jewelry

**Model**
- Free physical ring sizer available.
- Manufacturer warranty covers defects.
- **Triton Forever Fit Warranty™** provides lifetime size exchanges for wedding bands, subject to availability.
- If the original style is unavailable, customer care may substitute a similar style/value.
- Original purchaser only; not transferable.
- Third-party alterations can void warranty.
- Personalized/engraved rings have warranty limitations.

**Interesting tactic**
- Triton explicitly brands lifetime sizing as a named service: **Forever Fit**.

**Source**
- https://tritonjewelry.com/pages/customer-care
- https://tritonjewelry.com/pages/faqs

## 6. Thorum

**Model**
- Size exchanges use a $25 fee.
- Thorum sends the new ring first.
- A return label arrives with the replacement; customer then swaps the rings and sends the old ring back.
- Initial exchange must be started within 30 days.
- Their lifetime warranty can replace the original purchase one time with the same design, or a similar one if discontinued.

**Interesting tactic**
- **Advance replacement** dramatically reduces emotional friction: the customer is not left without a ring while waiting for the exchange to be processed.
- The $25 exchange fee is explained as covering shipping, repackaging, restocking, sanitizing, and polishing.

**Source**
- https://www.thorum.com/products/exchange

---

## 7. Element Ring Co.

**Model**
- Lifetime manufacturer warranty for non-wood carbon fiber, metal, and glass-fiber rings.
- Manufacturing defects can result in replacement.
- Size changes caused by weight gain/loss are explicitly excluded.
- Accidental abuse, chemical/fire exposure, loss, theft, and deliberate/accidental deformation are excluded.
- Wood rings have more limited warranty treatment.

**Interesting tactic**
- Very explicit list of what warranty **does not** mean, which reduces ambiguity.

**Source**
- https://elementringco.com/pages/warranty-policy

## 8. Revolution Jewelry

**Model**
- Strong incentive to use the brand's own sizing system.
- If the customer ordered and used Revolution ring sizers before production:
  - size change within 30 days: no fee
  - 31 days–1 year: 20% of current retail price
  - after 1 year: 40%
- If the customer did **not** use Revolution sizers:
  - within 30 days: 20%
  - after 30 days: 40%
- Second size exchange: 40%.
- Size exchange must remain the same style; no width/inlay redesign.
- Buyer pays return and replacement shipping.
- Separate loss/damage plans can be purchased.

**Interesting tactic**
- This is one of the strongest market examples supporting MONOLITH's Fit Kit strategy: **using the official sizing process earns better exchange economics**.

**Source**
- https://revolutionjewelry.com/pages/faqs
- https://revolution.helpscoutdocs.com/article/92-what-is-your-resizing-policy

---

## 9. Thorsten Rings

**Model**
- Lifetime sizing warranty for alternative-metal rings.
- If finger size changes, ring is replaced in the new size.
- Replacement is same style, not a different style.
- Engraving/custom work must generally be redone at the customer's expense.
- Small processing fee may apply after 45 days.
- Customer/retailer is responsible for shipping.
- Accidental breakage may be covered under warranty terms.

**Interesting tactic**
- Lifetime sizing is framed as a logical consequence of alternative metals being non-resizable.

**Source**
- https://www.thorstenrings.com/pages/warranty
- https://www.thorstenrings.com/pages/return-policy

## 10. Blue Nile

**Model**
- One free resize within the first year on eligible rings.
- Most eligible precious-metal rings can be adjusted within approximately two sizes.
- Alternative metals such as tungsten, titanium, and ceramic generally cannot be resized.
- Non-resizable rings may be eligible for exchange during the initial return window.
- Engraved/personalized items are generally excluded from return/exchange.

**Interesting tactic**
- Clear distinction between **resizing a resizable metal** and **replacing/exchanging a non-resizable alternative-metal ring**.

**Source**

- https://www.bluenile.com/policies/returns

# Patterns Across the 20 Brands

## Pattern A — Free initial correction, paid later correction

Seen in different forms at:

- Manly Bands
- Northern Royal
- James Allen
- Revolution Jewelry

This strongly supports MONOLITH's current direction of:

- first size exchange free
- additional size exchange paid

---

## Pattern B — Lifetime fit / replacement instead of physical resizing

Especially common with alternative-metal rings:

- Larson Jewelers
- Triton
- Thorsten
- Northern Royal
- Alpine Rings
- Ridge
- Tungsten Affinity

This is directly relevant because many MONOLITH rings will use materials such as tungsten that cannot be conventionally resized.

---

## Pattern C — Official sizer earns better protection

Most clearly demonstrated by:

- Revolution Jewelry

Also supported conceptually by brands that actively push proprietary sizers before exchange.

This validates the idea that MONOLITH can make its Fit Kit more than a measuring tool: it can be part of the customer's **Fit Assurance eligibility**.

---

## Pattern D — Limited number of lifetime replacements

Examples:

- Ridge: up to two replacements
- Alpine Rings: up to two replacements
- Thorum: one lifetime warranty replacement in its stated program

This creates predictable financial exposure for the brand while still sounding meaningfully generous.

---

## Pattern E — Deductible after free period

Examples:

- Northern Royal: $30 later size exchange; $45 later damage replacement
- Thorum: $25 exchange
- Tungsten Affinity: $25 deductible
- Thorsten: processing/service fees after initial period

This is a strong precedent for a MONOLITH post-benefit replacement contribution.

---

## Pattern F — Engraving is often the friction point

Competitors take several approaches:

1. **Engraving makes ring final sale**
   - Manly Bands
   - Blue Nile in return context

2. **Replacement is allowed, but engraving must be repurchased**
   - Larson
   - Thorsten

3. **Replacement includes/reproduces engraving**
   - Ridge
   - some Jared/Zales protection-plan scenarios

This is one of MONOLITH's largest opportunities to differentiate.

# Strongest Ideas for MONOLITH to Consider

These are research-derived recommendations, **not approved policy**.

## 1. Keep the Fit Kit economically meaningful

The $20 credited Fit Kit is sensible.

A stronger version could make official Fit Kit use part of the customer's enhanced protection eligibility, similar to Revolution's incentive model.

Example concept:

**Sized with MONOLITH Fit Kit**
- first wrong-size exchange free

**Skipped Fit Kit / self-sized**
- first exchange may still be available, but with different shipping or service terms

This would need owner approval before use.

---

## 2. Brand lifetime sizing separately from defect warranty

Market language strongly supports separating:

- **Manufacturing Warranty**
- **Lifetime Fit / Replacement Benefit**

Triton's Forever Fit, Ridge's Never Lost Forever Fit, Alpine's Evergreen Policy, and Thorsten's Lifetime Sizing Warranty all show that a named fit program can be understood independently from defect coverage.

---

## 3. A limited replacement count is easier to model financially

MONOLITH's current concept of **one lifetime replacement ring** is actually conservative compared with Ridge and Alpine, which advertise up to two replacement events.

This gives MONOLITH room to make the single included replacement feel generous while keeping lifetime liability controlled.

---

## 4. Consider reproducing MONOLITH engraving on the lifetime replacement

Ridge does this.

For a wedding band, reproducing the original engraving on a covered replacement has strong emotional logic because the inscription can be part of the meaning of the ring.

This is still an open MONOLITH decision.

---

## 5. The replacement-credit concept appears differentiated

Within this 20-brand set, several brands use:

- deductibles
- processing fees
- percentage replacement costs
- paid protection plans

But the specific MONOLITH concept of:

**pay a replacement contribution → receive replacement ring → same contribution remains as future MONOLITH store credit**

does not appear as the standard model among the competitors reviewed here.

That may become a distinctive retention mechanism if the economics and legal/accounting treatment are designed carefully.
